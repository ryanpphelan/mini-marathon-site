Volunteer

Help make event day happen

Our events run on volunteers. Lend a few hours and be part of the reason someone crosses the finish line.

Volunteer sign-up is connected to our system. While the site is in preview, please treat any entries as tests.

Why it matters

A few hours goes a long way

Setup and teardown, welcoming people at check-in, cheering at the finish, handing out water or pizza, taking photos: there’s a spot for every kind of helper, and no experience needed.

Tell us how you’d like to pitch in and when you’re free, and we’ll follow up with the details as the event gets closer. Groups and families are welcome too.

Questions first? Contact us and we’ll help.
