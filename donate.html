Your gift helps people take the next step

Help us bring hereditary cancer awareness, testing, and support to more communities.

Somewhere, a family is about to get the call.

The one that starts with two words: “It’s cancer.” For too many families, it comes too late — after the chance to know, to test, to act has already slipped by. Your gift is how we reach the next family in time.

It turns fear into a plan. It puts answers in someone’s hands before a diagnosis instead of after. And it means one more person gets to take 26.2 steps beside the people they love. When you give, you’re standing behind someone you may never meet, on the day they need it most.

You can’t change a family’s history. But you can change what happens next.

Where your money goes

Every gift has a clear path

At least 70% goes to care

At least 70% of every dollar we raise goes directly to our partner charities — the Kamie K. Preston Hereditary Cancer Foundation and the Nebraska Medicine Oncology Patient & Family Experience Fund — who put it to work for patients and families. See exactly where every dollar goes.

A small share keeps it running

A small percentage is kept to cover the real cost of putting on the event. No one on the Mini Marathon team draws a salary, and we’ll publish our allocation as we complete our 501(c)(3).

A formal annual allocation statement will be published as we complete our 501(c)(3). Because our 501(c)(3) status is still in formation, gifts are not tax-deductible at the present time. We’ll update this page as soon as that changes. For a plain-language breakdown of where every dollar goes, see our Full Transparency page.

Our 10-Year Goal

Help us raise $10,000 for our 10th year.

Our goal is $10,000: $5,000 from supporters and sponsors, matched dollar-for-dollar by a 100% board match. At least 70% goes straight to the Hereditary Cancer Foundation and Nebraska Medicine Oncology; the rest covers the cost of the event, and no one here draws a salary.

Get in touch to give

Online giving is coming soon. The giving form above is a preview of how donations will work once our platform is live. For now, you can give in person at any event or contact us to donate directly. No payment is processed on this page.
