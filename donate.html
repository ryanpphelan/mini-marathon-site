Your gift helps people take the next step

Help us bring hereditary cancer awareness, testing, and support to more communities.

Somewhere, a family is about to get the call.

The one that starts with two words: “It’s cancer.” For too many families, it comes too late — after the chance to know, to test, to act has already slipped by. Your gift is how we reach the next family in time.

It turns fear into a plan. It puts answers in someone’s hands before a diagnosis instead of after. And it means one more person gets to take 26.2 steps beside the people they love. When you give, you’re standing behind someone you may never meet, on the day they need it most.

You can’t change a family’s history. But you can change what happens next.

Where your money goes

Every gift has a clear path

On event day

100% of donations collected at the event are shared equally between the Kamie K. Preston Hereditary Cancer Foundation and the Nebraska Medicine Oncology Patient & Family Experience Fund.

Year-round

Gifts made here support the Mini Marathon’s own programs — awareness, education, and access to genetic testing — and help us reach more communities beyond the Omaha metro.

A formal annual allocation statement will be published as we complete our 501(c)(3). Donations may be tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law; please keep your receipt and consult your tax advisor.

The Next Ten Years

Help us reach more families over the next ten years.

Every gift helps a family move from fear to a plan — education, access to genetic testing, and the support to act on what they learn.
