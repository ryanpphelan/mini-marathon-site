Who We Walk For

Every step has a name.

For someone we lost. For someone in the fight right now. For a survivor who inspires us. This is who we walk for.

The Mini Marathon isn’t 26.2 miles — it’s 26.2 steps, taken in honor of someone. Some of us walk for a person we’ve lost. Some walk for a loved one in treatment right now. Some walk for a survivor, or for themselves. This is where we hold their names, and remember why we take the next step.
