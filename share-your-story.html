Share Your Story

Your story could be the reason someone takes the next step

A single story moves people to get tested, to walk, to give, more than any statistic can. If the Mini Marathon has meant something to you, we’d be honored to hear it.

Why we ask

Every story helps another family

Maybe you walked for someone you lost. Maybe getting tested changed everything. Maybe you’ve come back year after year and it’s become part of who you are. Whatever it is, telling it can reach a family who needs to hear it.

Here’s what happens next: we read every submission. If your story feels like a fit to share, someone from our team will reach out first, and nothing goes public without your say-so. You’re always in control of your own story.

This is a sensitive space, and we treat it that way. Share only what you’re comfortable sharing.
