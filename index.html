Our Cause

Hereditary cancer risk is often hidden — until it isn’t.

Too many people never learn that their cancer risk may be inherited, that genetic testing may be right for them, or that knowing early can change everything. We exist to close that gap and remove the barriers — fear, cost, access, and confusion — that stop people from taking the next step.

Awareness

Understand hereditary cancer risk, family history, and why genetic testing matters.

Empowerment

Get trusted information, real stories, and expert voices so you can make informed choices.

Action

Take the next step: testing, a provider conversation, or joining us at the event.

Denise’s Story
“Most marathons are for those dedicated souls. This one is dedicated to all souls.”

Denise Ibsen Cole founded the Mini Marathon after losing her mother to cancer and learning she carries the BRCA1 mutation. In 2016 she broke her hip training for the Chicago Marathon — and decided everyone deserves to cross a finish line. So: 26.2 steps, not miles.
